ESG & Market Dynamics

Predicting Stock Direction with Deep Learning

LSTM + Attention · 700 Stocks · Walk-Forward Validation

Why did we do this?

The Gap: Most financial ML relies solely on price-based technical indicators. We hypothesized that ESG scores encode latent risks and institutional sentiment that aren't captured by price alone.

Research Questions

  • Can non-traditional, qualitative data provide a verifiable edge over pure price models?
  • Does strong ESG compliance act as a buffer against downward price moves?
  • Can deep learning (LSTM + Attention) extract this signal from noisy market data?

What did we do?

Data Setup

  • 700+ US-listed stocks (2020 – 2023)
  • ESG scores (Environment, Social, Governance) from Finnhub
  • Daily OHLCV prices via Yahoo Finance
  • Binary label: price Up or Down in 5 trading days

Experimental Design

  • ESG Model — 28 features (price + ESG + derived)
  • Baseline — 9 price-only technical features
  • 3-seed ensemble for statistical stability
  • Strict chronological walk-forward split

How did we do it?

Part 1 — Overcoming the overfitting trap

Problem: Model reached 55 % but overfitted by epoch 5 — memorised training data, predicted "Up" almost exclusively.

Fixes Applied

FixEffect
Architecture Hidden 64→32, Layers 2→1Less memorisation capacity
Dropout 0.3 → 0.5Stronger regularisation
Balanced Sampler50/50 Up/Down per batch
Label Smoothing ε = 0.1No overconfident predictions
Grad Clip 0.5Stable weight updates

How did we do it?

Part 2 — Feature engineering & subsampling

Industry-Relative ESG

Converted raw scores to z-scores within each industry — captures whether a stock leads or lags its sector peers.

7 Window Features

MACD, Bollinger position, OBV momentum, skewness, kurtosis, trend strength, volatility trend — all computed within each 30-day window.

Sequence Subsampling

Adjacent windows overlap by 97 %. Keeping every 3rd sequence forces the LSTM to learn general patterns instead of memorising dates.
